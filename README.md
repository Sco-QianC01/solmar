SOLMAR is an original open-world crime game in the browser: Mara Voss arrives in the sun-soaked coastal city of Solmar with $150 and a name to make. Explore the city on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the police as your wanted level climbs, and work five story missions for the fixer Rico — from a first stolen car to the big score at the Marina casino. Taxi fares and hidden Sun Coins pay along the way.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. No network needed: every file the game loads lives under `game/`.

## Controls

- **WASD** move / drive · **Mouse** look · **Shift** run · **Space** jump
- **E** enter / exit a car, pick up, interact · **F** punch · **1** pistol / **2** fists
- **H** horn · **Esc / P** pause

## The city

A procedural 640 m coastal city: a downtown grid, a plaza, a mall, a marina, docks, and a beach. Traffic follows the roads, pedestrians walk the sidewalks and flee from trouble, and police cars and foot patrols respond to your wanted level. Progress, cash and mission state save to the browser.
